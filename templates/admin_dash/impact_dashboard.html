How to read this dashboard

Where the numbers come from

  • Participation and progress: each group’s Participants page (Grupos → Participantes), including uploaded historical groups.
  • Applicants: the application forms on this site.
  • Surveys: the initial and final participant surveys.
  • Website, social and course reach: Wix, our website analytics and the Marketing Dashboard. These are overall totals and do not change with the filters.

Limitations to keep in mind

  • Application and survey questions changed over time, so metrics only cover the groups named in each section. Older groups are not always comparable.
  • Progress statuses are entered by program staff on the Participants pages.
  • Groups still in progress are left out of graduation and dropout figures, because their outcome is not final yet.
  • People are matched across sources by email address. A person without an email on file cannot be matched.
