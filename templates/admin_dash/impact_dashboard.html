“Mentored in a later group” counts mentoras who repeated and emprendedoras who became mentoras, once per source participation. It checks all available linked-sheet and imported-group history beyond the selected group/year, matches email (or document when email is missing), and requires a later start date and evidence that they started as a mentora. Missing identity, dates or unrecorded future participation cannot be confirmed. Still active cross-references Activa participants in currently running groups across both roles and all years. It can overlap graduation or dropout. Milestones can overlap.

  • Participation and progress come from linked Google Sheets and completed historical imports on each group’s Participants page; applicants from the application forms; satisfaction from the initial and final surveys.
  • Application and survey questions changed over time, so each figure covers only the groups named beside it. Older groups are not always comparable.
  • Statuses are entered by program staff. People are matched across sources by email, so someone without an email on file cannot be matched.
